Privacy Policy

Effective November 12, 2026.

About Cozalume

Cozalume is independently operated. This Privacy Policy applies to the Cozalume iOS app and this support website. Contact support@kvvjvv.top with privacy questions or requests.

Photos on your device

You choose a photo through the system photo picker or camera. Cozalume uses it to prepare the feature you select and check image suitability. Local checks may examine face locations, visible body landmarks, lighting and image quality.

Photos may remain in memory while you use a feature. When the app archives a task, it stores a normalized input image, task information and any available result in the app’s local storage. Task information can include the selected style or color, task identifiers, status, errors and timestamps. The normalized image may differ from the source in your photo library.

Browsing reference images and choosing a photo do not themselves send anything off your device. A preview is only requested after you confirm the disclosure shown for that feature, and that confirmation is recorded separately from any other consent.

AI preview processing

Previews are produced by WaveSpeedAI, a third-party image processing service. When you start a preview, the photo you selected and a reference image for the selected style are sent to WaveSpeedAI, processed there, and the result is returned to Cozalume.

WaveSpeedAI processes this outside your country, so creating a preview transfers that photo out of your country. The app asks for your confirmation before the first preview for that reason, and you can refuse: previews then stay unavailable and the rest of the app keeps working.

Cozalume asks WaveSpeedAI to delete the uploaded photo and the generated preview as soon as your preview is ready. If that request fails, WaveSpeedAI keeps both for no longer than 7 days and then removes them. WaveSpeedAI states that it does not use submitted content to train its models.

The preview itself is stored on your device and, for your history, on Cozalume’s own server under an identifier for this install only — not under your name, email address or Apple ID.

Feedback and email support

The Feedback page keeps your type, message and optional screenshot in memory for the current app session. It does not automatically add your photo, generation result or task details. A selected screenshot is resized and encoded as a new JPEG without copying its source location or camera metadata. Review the image itself for personal details before attaching it.

In the current test version, Submit opens the system Mail composer with your message and the screenshot you selected. You can review, edit, cancel or send the email there. Your email account supplies the sender address. Mail may save a draft or queue a message; this does not confirm that support has received it. Cozalume keeps your Feedback draft after the composer closes. If Mail is not set up, you can copy the support address and use your preferred email app.

Email sent to support@kvvjvv.top is forwarded through Cloudflare Email Routing to a support inbox hosted by Google. Your sender address, message and any attachments are processed by the mail services involved in delivering and storing the correspondence.

Subscription information

The app includes subscription functionality supported by RevenueCat. It may process an app user identifier, purchase history, entitlement status and transaction information to check access and restore purchases. The app’s code does not receive your payment card number. Subscription screens are being tested; this does not establish that Apple purchases are available.

Storage, saving and deletion

Feedback drafts are held for the app session. Archived task images and records remain in the app’s local storage. Deleting a finished item in Me removes its local result and visible task record; it does not currently remove the archived input image.

Saving or sharing creates copies managed through your photo library or the apps and people you choose. Deleting an item in Cozalume does not delete the source in Photos, saved or shared copies, another service’s records or subscription information.

Support correspondence is stored in the support inbox. Deleting this install’s data in the app removes the server record tied to this install — its preview history and its consent record — and the app then starts over under a new install identifier. It does not delete previews you already saved to your photo library, copies you shared, or email you sent. For any other request concerning information held by Cozalume, email support@kvvjvv.top with enough information to identify your request. Avoid sending additional sensitive information unless it is needed to handle the request.

The website and service infrastructure

The website’s own page code contains no advertising, analytics, contact forms, photo uploads or payment processing. Hosting, domain and access services may process technical request information, such as IP addresses and browser request data, to deliver and protect the site.

Mail and infrastructure services have their own information-handling terms. You can read the Cloudflare Privacy Policy and Google Privacy Policy.

Changes and contact

This notice will be updated when the app or its services change. Privacy questions and requests: support@kvvjvv.top.
