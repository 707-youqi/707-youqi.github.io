Common questions
Why can I browse styles but not generate a preview?

The current review version lets you explore reference collections and prepare a photo. Cloud generation has not been enabled. A reference image is an example rather than a result made from your photo.

How do I send feedback?

In the current test version, write your message and optionally choose a screenshot, then tap Submit to open the system Mail composer. Review the message and tap Send in Mail. A saved or queued message does not confirm that support has received it. Your Feedback draft stays in the app. If Mail is not set up, copy support@kvvjvv.top and use your preferred email app.

What happens when I delete an item in Me?

Me removes that local result and its visible record. The archived input image, the source photo in Photos, any saved or shared copies, and remote or purchase records are handled separately. Contact support for a request concerning information held by the operator or another service.

How do I manage an Apple subscription?

If you purchased a subscription through Apple in a released version, use your Apple account to manage or cancel it. Deleting Cozalume does not cancel the subscription. See Apple's subscription instructions.

How do I request an Apple refund?

Use Apple's refund request process for eligible Apple purchases. A Refund message in Cozalume Feedback does not submit that request or guarantee approval.

How do I make a privacy request?

Email support@kvvjvv.top with enough information to explain your request and locate the relevant record. Do not send additional sensitive information unless it is necessary and requested through an appropriate channel. The current Privacy Policy describes local storage, email support and the limits of deleting an item in Me.
